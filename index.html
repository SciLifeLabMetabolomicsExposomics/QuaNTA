QuaNTA tutorial

QuaNTA (Quantitative & Nontargeted Analysis) turns an MS-DIAL alignment file into quality-checked, normalized and calibrated data, with reports and exported tables. This tutorial walks through how to structure your data, upload your data and how to interpret the app’s results one figure at a time: what each figure shows, how to read it, and what to do when something looks wrong.

All figures here are made by QuaNTA itself from the synthetic example dataset in example_data/, processed with the app’s default settings, so they look and behave like the figures in the app. They are interactive: hover over points for details, click legend entries to hide or show groups, and drag to zoom (double-click to zoom out).

Try it yourself

  1. Open QuaNTA on SciLifeLab Serve, or run it locally (see the README).
  2. Upload QuaNTA_example_ESIpos.zip and example_params.yml from example_data/ and click Read data file, then Process data.
  3. Follow the chapters in the menu; each names the section of the app it explains (e.g. 4.5).
Note

This tutorial matches the QuaNTA version in the same commit of the repository. Older versions of the app may differ.
